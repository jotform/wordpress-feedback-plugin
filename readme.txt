Add a floating feedback button to your WordPress site. Let visitors quickly submit comments, suggestions, and bug reports.

== Description ==

Jotform Feedback Button plugin makes it incredibly easy for visitors to share their thoughts without leaving the page. Instead of navigating to a separate contact form or pop-up, users can click a small floating button and submit feedback in seconds.

🟠 Jotform’s feedback button is designed for site owners who want real, actionable insights. The Jotform Feedback Button helps you gather comments, UI/UX issues, bug reports, suggestions, and general messages; all through a customizable interface.

The best part? The feedback button is powered by Jotform’s flexible form builder, which lets you tailor every detail, including fields, layout, colors, branding, notifications, and more.

= 💬 What does the Jotform Feedback Button plugin do? =

The plugin adds a floating feedback widget to your WordPress site, allowing visitors to submit feedback instantly.

With the plugin, you can:
- Create a personalized feedback form using Jotform
- Add a floating feedback button to any page
- Customize the button's appearance and placement
- Let visitors send feedback, bug reports, and suggestions easily
- Store and manage all submissions inside your Jotform account
- Receive instant alerts when new feedback comes in

It’s the simplest way to keep your website connected to your audience.

== Installation ==

1. Download repo's tarball/zip and extract it to your WordPress's Plugins folder ({wordpress-folder}/wp-content/plugins)
2. Enter your WordPress Administrator panel (http://my-wordpress.url/wp-admin)
3. Activate plugin under Plugins section
4. Go to settings page of plugin (Settings -> Jotform Feedback Button)
5. Enter your form ID (or select with magnifier icon from your Jotform account)
6. Customize as needed (colors, positions, button titles, etc.).
7. Save your settings
8. Hola, your feedback button is ready on all pages 🙂

== Frequently asked questions ==

= Is the Jotform Feedback Button customizable? =

Jotform is an online form builder with over 35 million users. Jotform provides the most customizable feedback buttons available anywhere. You will be able to leverage Jotform’s expertise with feedback buttons.

You can customize both the feedback button and the form inside. On the button, you can customize the button colors, font colors, and its location. The form inside is also completely customizable. You can customize the form title, the form questions, and the thank-you page shown after form submission.

= Do I need coding experience to create a feedback button? =

Not at all! You can set up your feedback button entirely through the WordPress dashboard or Jotform’s intuitive, no-code Form Builder. Simply customize your form, enter your Form ID in the plugin settings, and the button will appear on your site automatically. If you’d like more advanced styling, you can optionally add custom CSS, but it’s never required.

= Will I be notified when someone fills out my feedback form? =

You can easily set up [notification emails](https://link.jotform.com/cJZ6Eu19T4) in your form’s settings. Simply enter your email address into the recipients list, and you’ll instantly receive emails as soon as someone fills out your form! = How do I find my Jotform form ID? =

Your form ID is the string of digits at the end of your [form’s URL](https://link.jotform.com/EFO5TKlzus). If you like, you can [create a custom form URL](https://link.jotform.com/a1gHE6DpMU) that replaces these numbers.
